⚡ Quick Rules
Bunce! at a glance
  • Goal: score the most points after 6 rounds.
  • Setup: 2–4 players, each with their own colour deck of 27 cards (A–Z plus a ✳ wild). Player 1 goes first and can be the AI.
  • Rounds: each round you draw a fresh hand of 7, then 6, 5, 4, 3 and 2 cards. Unplayed cards are discarded.
  • First turn: spell any word of 3+ letters. It goes on the table.
  • Every turn after, connect to the table word:
    Crossword: build through one table letter. Your word replaces the table word.
    Extend off end/start: add one card to the table word, then build a new word across through it.
    Wrap (prefix, suffix or both): add letters to the table word's ends. The longer word stays.
  • Scoring: + cards in your word (including table letters it uses) · − cards you didn't play · +10 Bunce for using all your cards · Can't play = lose your whole hand's value.
  • Challenges: opponents tap Challenge or Pass. Valid = player gets +10 per challenger. Not valid = each challenger gets +10, and the player tries again.
  • Wild ✳: any letter you choose (worth 6).
  • Values: N R T 10 · D H L S 8 · B M P 6 · vowels 5 · C F G W 4 · J K V Y 2 · Q X Z 1
📖 Official Bunce! Rules
108 cards · 4 coloured sets · 6 rounds · 2–4 players

Objective

  • Build words and score the most points. The player with the highest total after 6 rounds wins.

Components & Setup

  • 108 cards: 4 coloured decks of 27 (A to Z plus one ✳ wild). Every card has a point value.
  • Add 2–4 players on the start screen. Player 1 plays the Gold deck, Player 2 Red, Player 3 Purple and Player 4 Teal. Each deck is shuffled for you.
  • You can make Player 1 an AI opponent (Easy, Medium or Hard).
  • Player 1 goes first. The game keeps score for you.
  • Use Merriam-Webster to settle challenges (there's a look-up button), or agree on another dictionary before you start.

  • Each round, every player takes one turn with a new hand drawn from their own deck.
  • If Player 1's very first hand has no vowel (a wild counts as one), the game shows a warning and deals them a new hand.
  • Turns go in order: Player 1, Player 2, and so on.

Playing a Turn

  • The first turn: Player 1 spells any word from their hand. It goes on the table. (The Play type buttons appear once there's a word to connect to.)
  • After that, your word must connect to the word on the table. Pick one of these play types:
  • Crossword: tap a letter on the table and build your word through it with cards from your hand (e.g. NAP through the A in SKATE). Your word replaces the table word, and the crossed letter counts toward your score.
  • Extend off end / Extend off start: add one card to the end or start of the table word so it makes a longer word (e.g. SKATE → SKATES). Then build a new word across through that card (e.g. SUN). Both words must be valid. The new word replaces the table word.
  • Prefix wrap / Suffix wrap / Both wrap: add letters to the start, the end or both ends of the table word (e.g. LIGHT → DELIGHTFUL). The longer word stays on the table, and all its letters, including the table's, count toward your score.
  • Only one word is on the table at a time.
  • Words must be 3 letters or longer. Any entry in the dictionary counts, including proper nouns and abbreviations without punctuation.
  • A wild card can be any letter you choose when you play it. If you cross through a wild on the table, you choose its letter.
  • Cards you don't play are discarded and count against you. They don't carry over to your next turn.
  • Can't play? Tap Can't play (twice to confirm). Your hand is discarded, you lose its total value, and the table word stays.

Challenges & the Bunce Bonus

  • Before points are counted, each opponent taps Challenge or Pass. If everyone passes, the points are counted straight away.
  • If someone challenges, look the word up (📖 Look up on Merriam-Webster) and tap Valid or Not valid.
  • Valid: the word player earns +10 Bunce for each challenger, plus their normal word score.
  • Not valid: each person who challenged earns +10 Bunce. The player picks up their cards and tries again.
  • The AI opponent challenges any word that isn't in its 20,000-word list. You make the ruling.
  • A Bunce is also awarded automatically when a player uses all of their drawn cards in one turn.

Card Colours & Values

  • Each player plays from their own coloured deck: Gold, Red, Purple or Teal.
  • Every deck has the same 27 cards (A to Z plus one ✳ wild) with the same values. A card's value is the number in its top-left corner.
  • 10: N R T · 8: D H L S · 6: B M P ✳ · 5: A E I O U · 4: C F G W · 2: J K V Y · 1: Q X Z
